## Copy Pattern To Reuse Safely

- Lead with a direct earning or product promise.
- Follow with trust/security proof.
- Explain the plan in simple numbers.
- Show live-looking activity tables.
- Add social proof through testimonials.
- Offer a clear account/deposit/withdraw flow.
- Add referral income as a growth loop.
- Close with security, supported exchanges, and Bitcoin-only payment rails.

## Caution For Our Use

The source site repeatedly makes very aggressive financial claims, including guaranteed returns, zero risk, and tax-free earnings. If we reuse this style, we should be careful with compliance, disclaimers, and jurisdiction-specific financial rules.
